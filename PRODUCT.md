# Bible Brain® — Product Context

## What it is
Bible Brain® is a **structured Scripture engagement platform for teenagers** developed by Scripture Brain Limited (Nigeria). Not a one-off church programme. A long-term, multi-generational system — designed to run 100 years without content repetition through five integrated modules (TV show, daily home play, weekly home play, champion-of-champions, decade-cycle Bible Ultimate Search).

## Register
**brand** — `biblebrain.ng` is a marketing site. The site IS the product surface that converts sponsors and partners. Not a product UI, not a dashboard. Editorial, identity-driven, sponsor-facing.

## Users (in order of importance for THIS site)

1. **Prospective sponsors** receiving physical letters from Collins (founder) and visiting the site to verify legitimacy before responding. Read the letter, check the site, decide whether to forward to their org's CSR/foundation lead. **The site exists primarily to win this 5-second credibility check.**
2. **Faith institutions** (churches, schools, denominations) considering institutional partnership.
3. **Media/broadcast partners** evaluating program-licensing or distribution deals.
4. **Teenagers and their families** following the brand on social, looking up event details, watching highlight clips. Lower priority for v1 — they're already engaged via socials and live events.

Sponsors are reading the site **on a phone**. Most receive Collins's letter, then check the URL on their mobile during commute or downtime. Mobile experience is not a nice-to-have; it's the primary visit context.

## Product purpose
Convert credibility into action. The visitor finishes the letter or the social post wondering "is this real?" The site answers yes through:
- Track record proof (₦500K → ₦575K → ₦1M+ prize awards 2023→2026; 100% executed)
- Authentic event documentation (32 photos of real teenagers, real prize presentations)
- A 100-year strategic frame that reads as serious infrastructure, not seasonal evangelism
- Direct human contact (WhatsApp + email + four social channels) with a real Nigerian phone number

Then it routes them: read the platform overview → request the partnership deck → email Collins.

## Voice and tone
**Confident, structured, faith-grounded, never preachy.** The brand sells legitimacy through specificity: 4,200 episodes, 36,525 days, ₦1,000,000+, exact module names. Numbers do the persuading; adjectives stay restrained.

Never use the language of evangelism aimed at teenagers ("get on fire for God", "epic Bible quest"). The voice is institutional and scriptural, addressed to adults with capital to deploy.

**Strong yes:** *"Bible Brain® is a structured, long-term Scripture engagement platform designed to run for 100 years without content repetition."*

**Strong no:** *"Discover how Bible Brain® is transforming faith for the next generation!"* — generic SaaS-marketing hype.

## Anti-references
- **Western megachurch landing pages** (vibrant orange CTAs, hyped headlines, "Watch a recent message"). Bible Brain is more institutional than expressive.
- **NGO charity sites** (begging bowls, pity photos, donation thermometers). Bible Brain is a platform, not a fundraiser; sponsors get measurable youth-engagement ROI, not warm fuzzies.
- **Generic SaaS** (gradient hero, three feature cards, testimonial slider). The site sells a programme, not software.
- **Crypto/fintech aesthetics** (glowing buttons, dark gradients, sparkles). Bible Brain is faith-anchored, not techno-utopian.
- **AI-slop landing pages** (hero metric template, identical card grids, gradient text, glassmorphism). The visual register must read as designed-by-humans-with-taste, not assembled-from-trending-dribbble.

## Strategic principles
1. **Photo authenticity beats stock.** Use Collins's 32 real-event photos (winner check presentations, kids reading Bibles, group portraits). Never stock images.
2. **Numbers carry the persuasion.** 4,200 / 36,525 / 5,200 / ₦1M+ are unique and credible. Foreground them in display type. Don't bury in body copy.
3. **Two-layer information architecture.** Layer 1 (About / Programmes / Events) is for any visitor. Layer 2 (Platform Overview / Partner With Us) is for serious sponsors who clicked through. The deeper they go, the more rigorous the content gets.
4. **Mobile is the primary context.** Phone visit > desktop visit > tablet. Design starts at 375×667 and earns its way up.
5. **Direct contact > contact form.** Real Nigerian WhatsApp number visible everywhere. Real email. The form is supplementary.
6. **Editorial restraint.** Plenty of breathing room, generous type, single-column rhythm. Avoid card-stuffing the homepage.

## Surface map

- `/about` — AboutHero → Vision/Mission → PlatformOverview (5 modules) → OurModel → WhatMakesUsUnique → ProvenGrowth → PartnershipCTA (Layer 2 deep dive)
- `/partner` — PartnerHero → PartnerOpportunities (4 tiers) → PartnerGains → PartnershipCTA (Layer 2 conversion)

## Status / scope
v1 is a pre-launch alignment with Collins's canonical brief. After launch, the partnership deck PDF will be added; testimonials follow when real ones come in. Out of scope: i18n, blog, analytics dashboard, donor payments, sponsor portal.
